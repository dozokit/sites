---
id: google-maps
title: Show up on Google Maps
when: they ask how to appear on Google Maps, Google search results with their hours, Google reviews, or Google Business Profile
---
Google Maps shows businesses that have a free **Google Business Profile**.

1. Go to [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business) and add your business (or claim it if it's already listed). Google checks you're the owner, usually by text, a phone call or a short video.
2. Fill in your hours, phone, photos and your website address: paste your site's address.
3. Once it's live, open your profile on Google Maps, click **Share** and copy the link. Paste it in **Settings → Google Business Profile** here: your site gets **Directions** and **Leave a review** buttons that use it.
