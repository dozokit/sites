---
id: booking
title: Let customers book online
when: they want online booking, appointments, reservations, a calendar, let customers pick a time slot, or their old site has a booking tool or widget (RestoPro, TheFork, Zenchef, Planity, Calendly, Doctolib)
---
**You already take bookings online** (RestoPro, TheFork, Zenchef, Planity, Calendly, Doctolib…)? Keep that tool: your site gets a **Book** button that opens your booking page, so nothing changes for you or your customers. Give Claude the link to your booking page (the one customers land on) and it adds the button. Booking widgets can't be built into the page itself, since sites only run their own scripts, but a button to your booking page works with every tool.

**No booking tool yet?** Use **Cal.com**: free for one person, it syncs with your Google or Outlook calendar.

1. Create a free account on [cal.com](https://cal.com) and connect your calendar.
2. Create an event type (for example "Haircut, 30 min") with your available hours, and copy its link.
3. Ask Claude "add a Book button with this link" and paste it: it adds a **Book** button to your page.
