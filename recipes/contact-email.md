title: Get your contact messages
when: they ask where contact form messages go, want messages or a notification by email, want a contact form or a contact button, or ask how customers can write to them
---
Your site can have a contact form and a **Write to us** button: ask Claude "add a contact form". Every message lands in **Messages** in Dozokit Sites, with the name, email and phone of the person.

To also get each message by email (free, about 2 minutes):

1. Open **Settings** (top right) **→ Connections → Messages by email** and type the email where you want your messages.
2. Click **Connect Resend**. Resend opens: sign up with that same email if you don't have an account, then click **Allow**.
3. Back here, click **Send a test** and check your mailbox.

Each message then arrives in your mailbox: press Reply to answer the visitor. Resend's free plan sends up to 100 emails a day.
