Use **Dozokit Analytics**: free, no cookie banner needed, and it runs in your own Cloudflare account like this site.

1. Open [dozokit.com/analytics](https://dozokit.com/analytics) and click **Deploy to Cloudflare**. Keep the suggested names and choose a password.
2. Open your new stats site, go to **Settings**, add your site's address and copy the **site id** it shows.
3. In Dozokit Sites, open **Settings** (top right) **→ Connections → Visitors**, paste your stats address and save. Then in your site's **Site settings → Visitors**, paste its site id.

From then on you see visits, where they come from, and each contact message as a goal.
